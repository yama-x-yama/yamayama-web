Day Maxer Support

Day Maxer is an iPhone app for planning your day and reviewing how you spent it. Contact support@ourpins.app for help. Please mention “Day Maxer” and your app version. Do not email passwords, Apple verification codes, or private schedule details.

Planning

Use Today to see the 24-hour ring and add plans. Your plans and actual activity records are stored on your iPhone. Review shows daily, weekly, and monthly summaries.

Recording activities

On Today, use either of the two recording rows to choose an activity and tap Start. Pause or resume as needed; Stop saves the measured time as an Actual record. The two rows can record overlapping activities on separate tracks. Use Edit to change or delete plans and activity records. Calendar shows daily rings and selected-day Plans and Actual lists.

Currently unavailable features

AI text and voice input, Sleep Mode, Early Clock, and device-calendar integration are currently hidden. Existing local records are retained.

Notifications

Allow notifications in iOS Settings to receive plan reminders. If you change a plan, open Day Maxer again to refresh scheduled notifications.

Account deletion

If you used an older release with an account, open Day Maxer Settings and choose Delete account. You will confirm with Sign in with Apple. This deletes the server account; local plans and activity records remain on the device. If the action fails, contact support@ourpins.app.
